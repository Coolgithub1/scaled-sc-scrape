# SC County BZA/ZBA/BOZA public roster sources

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Scope:** 14 empty counties + thin/name-quality boards listed below.  
**Rule:** names below are quoted from a public source. Unnamed seats are vacant or unpublished, not invented.

**MatchBoard sweep:** `GET https://api.matchboard.tech/app/entities` lists all 46 SC counties (Abbeville=1 … Williamsburg=45). `GET https://api.matchboard.tech/app/boards` returns 186 boards. Published zoning-appeals boards exist only for Beaufort, Calhoun, Clarendon, Colleton, Georgetown, Kershaw, Orangeburg, plus several municipalities. **None of the 14 empty counties have a MatchBoard BZA board.** Orangeburg is the only target county with a MatchBoard BZA (`/app/boards/139`).
